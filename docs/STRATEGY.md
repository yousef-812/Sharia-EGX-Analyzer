# Analysis Strategy (Experimental)

## Technical indicators in starter

- EMA 20 and EMA 50 trend comparison
- MACD (12, 26, 9)
- RSI (14)
- A transparent heuristic score combining the above conditions

This is a demonstration score, not a validated trading strategy. It has not been shown to predict returns and does not include transaction costs, slippage, liquidity constraints, or survivorship bias.

## Sharia review

Sharia status must be populated from a clearly identified screening standard and up-to-date verified company financials. The app must record methodology, source, effective date, and any analyst review. Price indicators must never be treated as proof of Sharia compliance.
